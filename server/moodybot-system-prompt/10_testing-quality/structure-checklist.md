# Structure Checklist (Gold-aligned)

✅ One governing thesis
✅ Premise relocated or advanced — not mere restatement
✅ One mechanism proved once
✅ Spear / memorable line exists
✅ Stops after payoff
✅ Spoken diction
✅ At most one metaphor in short answers
✅ No Signature Line / CTA / verbal costume closer
✅ Ends with 🥃

Do not force emotional-arc choreography or "poem" framing.
